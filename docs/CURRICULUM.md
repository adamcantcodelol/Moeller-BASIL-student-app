# Curriculum

## Goal

Turn BASIL computational biology modules into a single guided research
experience.

---

## 00 — Protein / PDB Setup

Students select a protein structure.

They learn:

- what a PDB entry is
- chains
- residues
- structural metadata
- basic protein visualization

Deliverable:

A verified protein structure.

---

## 01 — SPRITE

Students investigate structural/sequence relationships using the SPRITE
workflow defined by the BASIL curriculum.

Deliverable:

SPRITE results and observations.

---

## 02 — BLAST

Students compare protein sequences.

They learn:

- homology
- similarity
- sequence conservation
- alignment

Deliverable:

Relevant sequence comparisons.

---

## 03 — InterPro

Students identify:

- domains
- families
- signatures
- functional regions

Deliverable:

Functional annotation evidence.

---

## 04 — CLEAN

Students perform the CLEAN analysis defined by BASIL.

Deliverable:

CLEAN results and observations.

---

## 05 — Dali

Students compare structures.

They learn:

- structural similarity
- conserved folds
- structural alignment

Deliverable:

Relevant structural matches.

---

## 06 — Foldseek

Students perform rapid structural similarity searches.

Deliverable:

Structural homologs and observations.

---

## 07 — Active-Site Evidence Synthesis

Students combine evidence from earlier modules.

They should ask:

- Which residues are conserved?
- Which residues occur in relevant structural regions?
- Which residues appear functionally important?
- Which evidence agrees?
- Which evidence conflicts?

Deliverable:

Evidence-supported candidate active site.

---

## 08 — SwissDock

Students investigate ligand docking.

They learn:

- docking
- binding poses
- interactions
- limitations of computational predictions

Deliverable:

Docking observations.

---

## 09 — Hypothesis Builder

Students formulate their own hypothesis.

The system provides evidence.

The student provides the conclusion.

---

## 10 — ShannonBot Review

Students discuss their reasoning with ShannonBot.

The bot asks questions and identifies gaps.

---

## 11 — Reports

Students generate a research report.

The report should contain enough information for another person to understand
what was done and what evidence was obtained.
